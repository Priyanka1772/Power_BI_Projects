## Job Ad Insights Dashboard - Power BI Project
## Project Summary
* This Power BI dashboard offers a dynamic analysis of job advertisement data, focusing on key trends in job titles, contract types, and no. of employees.
* It aims to provide comprehensive insights for recruiters, job seekers, and HR professionals to better understand the job market landscape.

## Features
* Job Market Overview: Presents a clear summary of job ad metrics, including total job postings, distribution by contract type, and key trends in employee demand.
* Dynamic Filtering: Includes interactive slicers for Job Title, Contract Type, and Employee count etc.., allowing users to quickly filter and pinpoint specific job market segments.
* Date-Range Analysis: Features intuitive date sliders that enable precise analysis of job ad trends over custom time periods, revealing seasonality or shifts in demand.
* Detailed Report View: Provides a structured report view to deep-dive into the data, offering granular insights into job ad specifics and associated metrics.
* Customizable Insights: Users can explore relationships between various dimensions (e.g., how specific job titles relate to certain contract types, or employee demand over time) to uncover hidden patterns.

## Technologies Used
* Microsoft Power BI Desktop: For data modeling, visualization, and interactive dashboard creation.
* Microsoft Power BI Service: For publishing and understanding pipeline and dataflows.

## How To Access
* The Power BI report (.pbix) file for this project has been uploaded to this repository.
* Download the file and open it with Microsoft Power BI Desktop to explore the interactive dashboard locally.
