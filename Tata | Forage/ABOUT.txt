## Tata Forage - Retail Revenue Data Analysis
## Project Overview
* Recently participated in Tata's job simulation on the Forage platform, and it was incredibly useful to understand what it might be like to participate on a consulting team at Tata.
* Worked on a project to help a retail client review their revenue data to prepare for the future. 
* Created data visuals along with a home page, practiced using Tableau and PowerBI to represent information, and built my presentation skills in a real-world context.
 
## Features
* Interactive Revenue Dashboard: Explore key revenue trends and patterns over time and by various dimensions.
* Geographic Sales Analysis: Visualizes revenue performance across different regions or locations.
* Client-Focused Visuals: Designed to present complex data clearly for strategic business decisions.

## Technologies Used
* Power BI Desktop: For data visualization, report views, and home page designing.
